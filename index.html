Yay! and Agora.io (2025 - 2026)

Yay! is one of Japan's most well-known voice and video calling apps, operated by Nanameue, Inc. As of July 2026, it has reached a cumulative total of 12 million downloads. While the app has a long history and maintains an active develop department, it is also notorious for having a claim department that is extremely unresponsive to complaints. Furthermore, the app utilizes Agora.io, a developer platform that enables the integration of real-time communication features into applications and web services. However, the app itself contains numerous vulnerabilities.
